Warband Vault

Your characters. Their treasures. One vault.

Keep track of your characters’ belongings in one inventory browser for World of Warcraft: Forever. Find which character owns an item, browse recorded bank contents, and check your gold without switching characters.

Features

  • Character overview: Browse your recorded characters with portraits, class colors, levels, and realm names.
  • Gold tracking: See each character’s wallet balance and your combined recorded gold.
  • Inventory views: Browse bags, character banks, equipped items, and mailbox attachments.
  • Account-wide search: Search recorded items by name, item ID, character, realm, or item type.
  • Quality filters: Narrow your results by item quality.
  • Item details: Hover over an item to see its tooltip, recorded owners, quantities, and saved time.
  • Custom interface: Enjoy original vault artwork, a movable window, and adjustable scale.
  • Minimap shortcut: Open the vault with its custom minimap icon, drag it to your preferred position, or hide it with a command.

Getting started

  1. Install and enable Warband Vault Forever.
  2. Restart WoW after the first installation.
  3. Log into each character you want to record.
  4. Open each character’s bank and mailbox to save those contents.
  5. Type /wvf or click the vault icon near your minimap.

How tracking works

The addon records each character while you play them. Those saved records remain available when you switch to another character on the same local WoW account.

Bank and mailbox records update when their owner opens those locations. Saved times show how recently each location was recorded. Characters you have not logged into with the addon enabled will not appear yet.

Your current character uses their live portrait. Offline characters use race and gender portraits. Gold totals include character wallets; mailbox gold is displayed separately.

Commands

  • /wvf — Open or close the vault.
  • /wvf scan — Refresh accessible records.
  • /wvf minimap — Show or hide the minimap shortcut.
  • /wvf resetui — Reset the window’s position and scale.

Early alpha

This first version focuses on character inventories. Shared account banks, guild banks, auctions, currencies, and synchronization between computers are not included.
