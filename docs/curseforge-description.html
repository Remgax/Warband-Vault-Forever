Warband Vault Forever

Keep track of your characters' items and gold in World of Warcraft: Forever. Browse saved inventories, find supplies on your alts, and plan crafting without switching characters.

Your characters, one searchable vault

  • Browse recorded bags, banks, and mail across your characters.
  • Search for items and see which characters own them and where they are stored.
  • Use character and item filters to narrow down what you need.
  • See recorded gold balances and when inventory information was last updated.

Plan crafts and gather materials

Browse saved recipes and compare their material requirements with supplies recorded on your characters. See what you already have, what needs collecting from storage or alts, and what is still missing.

Craft through the game's own profession controls. Optional Auctionator integration helps turn missing materials into shopping lists and provides estimates from available cached prices.

Make it your own

Choose an illustrated or simpler interface style, adjust the window scale, and customize shortcuts, sound, and visual effects in Settings.

Optional account sharing

Pair compatible addon installations to exchange recorded characters between accounts through in-game addon messages. Pairing lets you browse shared records alongside your own characters.

Keep pairing codes private. Sync does not require your WoW password or an external cloud account. Messages are authenticated but are not encrypted. Communication and material transfers remain subject to the game's rules and account reachability.

Getting started

  1. Install and enable Warband Vault Forever, then restart the game after the first installation.
  2. Type /wvf or use an addon shortcut to open the vault.
  3. Log into each character you want to record. Visit their bank and mailbox to record those contents.
  4. Open their professions to save recipe information for crafting plans.

Use /wvf settings to customize the addon and /wvf help for in-game guidance.

About saved records

The vault shows recorded information, not a live view of offline characters. Bank and mailbox records refresh when their owner visits those locations. Check saved times and update older records before relying on material counts or making purchases.

Transfer restrictions affect which supplies can be used. Auction-house estimates depend on cached prices and may be incomplete or out of date. The addon does not automatically move items or buy auctions.
