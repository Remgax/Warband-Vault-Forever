Warband Vault Forever

Browse your characters' saved bags, banks, equipment, mail, and gold in World of Warcraft: Forever. Search for an item to see who owns it without switching characters.

First beta: 0.3.0-beta.1. Track your characters' items and plan crafts from the profession window. Download the first beta and report problems through the GitHub issue tracker.

Features

  • A character list with class colors, levels, realms, and gold.
  • Separate views for bags, character banks, equipment, and mailbox attachments.
  • Search by item name, item ID, character, realm, or item type. Filter by item quality.
  • Item tooltips with owner counts and saved times.
  • Character search with class, profession, realm, faction, and level filters.
  • Warband recipes in the profession window: saved character recipes, search, profession filters, bag availability, and quantity planning.
  • A collapsible materials panel beside native recipes, with bags, banks, alt holdings, saved times, and transfer restrictions.
  • Missing-material checks show which alts hold supplies and where they are stored.
  • Craft with the game's own Create and Create All controls. Build a materials list showing what to collect and what is still missing.
  • Auctionator shopping lists for remaining auction-house purchases, with rough costs from cached unit prices and clear price coverage.
  • An open leather satchel with stitched edges, brass fittings, and item hover glow.
  • A short opening animation and WoW's backpack-opening sound, with separate controls to turn them off.
  • A separate Settings menu with sliders for window scale, opening-sound volume, and effects.
  • A vault button beside the action-bar bags and a draggable minimap shortcut.
  • Optional account-level sync: pair two accounts once and share their recorded characters, inventories, banks, and gold.

Getting started

  1. Install and enable Warband Vault Forever.
  2. Restart WoW after the first installation.
  3. Log into each character you want to record.
  4. Open each character’s bank and mailbox to save those contents.
  5. Open their professions to save recipes. Expand categories and clear profession filters to record more recipes on legacy windows.
  6. Type /wvf or click the vault beside the action-bar bags or minimap.

How tracking works

The addon records each character while you play them. Those saved records remain available when you switch to another character on the same local WoW account.

Bank and mailbox records update when their owner opens those locations. Saved times show how recently each location was recorded. Characters you have not logged into with the addon enabled will not appear yet.

Gold totals include character wallets; mailbox gold is displayed separately.

Crafting and auction-house shopping

Open a profession from your spellbook. The Warband materials panel beside the native recipe view shows bags, bank and alt holdings, record ages, supplies to collect and quantities still missing. It follows the native recipe and quantity. Craft with the game's own Create and Create All controls.

The Warband recipes side tab lets you browse saved characters and professions, search recipes, filter by bag availability and plan a quantity. Show recipe returns a current-character recipe to the native page; open its profession first. Other characters' recipes remain available for planning. This integration requires ForeverCore profession tabs minor 2 or later.

Materials and shopping shows what to collect and buy. Use Create Auctionator list there to export missing purchases, then visit an auctioneer to buy them. Cost estimates use cached prices; unknown prices are excluded and partial totals are labelled.

Link another account

  1. Install this version on both accounts and log into both on the same realm and faction.
  2. On the first account, open Settings → Account sync → Create / show code.
  3. Copy the code privately to the other account. Paste it into Account sync and click Pair account.
  4. On the first account, choose Approve account.

Pairing applies to the whole account. Switching characters needs no new pairing. Both accounts exchange their own saved characters while online; offline records remain available with their original saved times. Bank contents update when their owner visits a banker. Large transfers may take time.

Sync Settings includes automatic updates, Sync now, profession and mailbox sharing, and Unlink. Unlink removes the pairing and imported records on that account. Already received records remain when a sharing option is turned off. One other account can be paired at a time.

Keep the pairing code private. Sync uses authenticated WoW addon messages and does not use your WoW password or a cloud service. Messages are not encrypted. Cross-realm and cross-faction discovery are not supported. Account sync passes simulated transfer and alt-switch tests; broader in-game reconnect testing continues during beta.

Commands

  • /wvf: open or close the vault.
  • /wvf scan: save accessible items.
  • /wvf settings: open settings.
  • /wvf sync: open account pairing and sync settings.
  • /wvf crafting: open Warband recipes in an already open profession window.
  • /wvf minimap: show or hide the minimap shortcut.
  • /wvf actionbar: show or hide the button beside the bags.
  • /wvf sound: turn the opening sound on or off.
  • /wvf effects: turn animation and hover glow on or off.
  • /wvf resetui: reset the window's position and scale.

Beta notes

Updating from alpha keeps your saved characters and settings. Install the beta over the existing addon folder and use /reload. Use the same addon version on both accounts for sync.

The profession layout, recipe browsing, material display, and return to native recipes have been checked in-game. Full crafting batches, Auctionator shopping, and two-account reconnects still need broader beta testing.

Shared account banks, guild banks, auctions, and currencies are not tracked.

For bug reports, include the addon version, client build, reproduction steps, and any Lua error. Keep pairing codes and account SavedVariables private.
